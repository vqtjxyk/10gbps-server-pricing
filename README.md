## Why 10gbps server prices range from $141 to $1,500 a month

The spread comes down to one question: is the 10Gbps port metered or unmetered?

Most "cheap 10Gbps" listings you'll find are metered. The port can burst to 10Gbps, but your monthly transfer is capped — typically 50TB or 100TB — and going over costs extra or gets throttled. Unmetered listings remove that cap entirely and price the plan around the sustained port speed instead, which is why they cost several times more.
